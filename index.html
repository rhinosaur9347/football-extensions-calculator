Rising Phoenix Football 2026 • Methodology: extension value = average salary of ranks N−3 through N+3 at the selected position; franchise tag = average of the top 5 salaries at the selected position • Franchise Tag override: if a player's current salary already exceeds the position's franchise tag value, the franchise tag is instead 120% of the player's current contract • Players may be franchised for a maximum of two years, and only in consecutive seasons.
